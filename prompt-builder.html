How to Write Better AI Prompts: Context, Task, Format

2Change the words to fit you

Type right in the boxes. Only Goal is required. Your prompt updates as you type.

What you want it to do. One clear job. Start with a verb: draft, summarize, explain, plan, compare.

What it should know first. Background, the situation, anything you'd tell a coworker before handing this off.

Who it's for. Who reads or uses the result. This sets the tone.

What it should look like. Bullets, a table, a short email, an outline, five sentences. Be specific.

How to keep it honest. Tell it to flag guesses or ask questions, so you catch AI making things up.

Why this works

Most bad AI answers come from vague prompts, not a bad tool. When you name the goal, the context, the audience, the format, and the check, you stop making the AI guess. Same recipe works in any tool. Learn it once, use it forever.
