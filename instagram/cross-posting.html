01

Why One App Takes Off

It isn't a glitch.

Facebook and Instagram are different audiences. Instagram trial reels go to people who don't follow you first, so a trial can do well even with a small following. When everything auto-shares, both apps get the same caption at the same time, and your comments split across two places you may not be checking.

One thing to remember

A reel doing well on one app and not the other isn't a glitch. It's two different audiences.
