Why Filter

Your comments are your storefront.

When a new visitor opens your post and the first comments are crypto offers and "DM me" bots, your post looks abandoned. Instagram has built-in filters that move those comments into a hidden section you can review. Nobody gets blocked, and nobody gets notified.

Do Not Touch

Filters should catch spam, not your customers.

  • Don't turn comments off. A post with no comments loses the conversation that helps it travel.
  • Don't add normal words like "link," "price," or "where" to your list. Real customers use them.
  • Don't block someone over one odd comment. Hide it, or use Restrict if it keeps happening.
  • Check hidden comments once a week. Open a post's comments, scroll to the bottom, and tap View hidden comments. Real people land there sometimes.
