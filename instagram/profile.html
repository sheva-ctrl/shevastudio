01

Why Visits Don't Turn Into Follows

The answer has to be on screen right away.

Someone sees one of your reels and taps your name. If your profile doesn't quickly answer "what do I get if I follow?", they leave. Check your starting point first, so you can see the change.

02

Four Fixes

In order. The first two matter most.

Fix 1 — the Name field
Fix 2 — three pinned posts
Fix 3 — highlights
Fix 4 — the bio
Set expectations

A clear profile turns more of your existing visits into follows. It won't create visits on its own — that's still your content's job.
