Why Emotes

Emotes are your LIVE's inside jokes.

An emote is a tiny picture your fans drop in your LIVE chat instead of typing. When your regulars can say "THANK YOU" or "ME TOO" with one tap, chat moves faster and new viewers can see there's a community here.

Who gets which emotes
  1. Fan emotesFor anyone in your Fan Club. Viewers join by sending you one Heart Me gift (1 Coin) during a LIVE.
  2. Super Fan emotesFor paid Super Fans. TikTok sets the price at $9.99 a month.
  3. Hidden comment emotesBuilt-in codes anyone can type in regular comments. Bonus in Step 4.
One thing to remember

Older tutorials say Creator tools → Subscription → Emotes. That changed on September 15, 2025. Custom emotes now live in your Fan Club, in two trays: Fan and Super Fan.

Get Them Used

Emotes nobody knows about don't get used.

Move 1 — say it out loud

Do Not Touch

Skip these and your emotes get approved the first time.

  • Don't follow tutorials that send you to Creator tools → Subscription. That menu moved in September 2025.
  • Don't use characters, logos, or art you don't own. It's the fastest way to get rejected.
  • Don't shrink a whole graphic into an emote. Small text turns to mush.
  • Don't launch emotes the day of a big LIVE. Review takes days.
  • Don't put all your best ones in the paid tray. Free fans need a reason to stay, too.
