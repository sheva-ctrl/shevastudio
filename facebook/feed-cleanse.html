01

Why Facebook Is Different

Gentler tools than Instagram.

On Instagram, cleanup means unfollowing. Facebook gives you gentler tools. Unfollow hides someone's posts but keeps you friends. Snooze hides them for 30 days. Favorites puts the people you care about first. None of these notify anyone.

One thing to remember

Unfollow is not unfriend. You stay friends, and they can still see what you post. You just stop seeing theirs.
