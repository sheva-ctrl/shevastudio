The Blue Follow Button

Free. Anyone can turn it on. Creator-ready in three switches.

Step 1 — the main switch
Turn on public followers Settings & privacy → Audience & Visibility → Followers & Public Content → Who Can Follow Me → Public Nothing else works without this. Once it's on, non-friends see a Follow button.
Step 2 — the part most creators miss
Make Follow the default Settings & privacy → Privacy → Who can send friend requests? → Friends of Friends Pushes Facebook to show Follow first instead of Add Friend.
Step 3 — front and center
Turn on Professional Mode Profile → three dots → Turn on Professional Mode Makes the profile look creator-ready and puts Follow up top.
One thing to remember

The blue Follow button and the blue verified badge are not the same. Follow is free and anyone can switch it on. Verification is a whole different process.
