How to use this ▾
  1. Pick a challenge to start, or hit Blank Canvas to build from scratch.
  2. Click any slide in the preview to select it. Edit it in the panel on the left.
  3. Toggle Light (the habit) or Dark (the superpower). Use Add or Delete to change the deck length.
  4. Keep the rhythm: a dark slide hooks with the superpower, the next light slide delivers the habit.
  5. Hit Download All Slides as PNG when you love it. Markdown and JSON save your text to reuse later.
