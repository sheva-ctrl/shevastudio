ALT TEXT
BUILDER

Some of your followers can't see your photos. Their phone reads the screen out loud, and when it reaches a photo, it reads the alt text.

Skip it and they hear the app's guess, like "May be an image of 1 person." This builder writes something better from four quick answers.

By the end: you'll have alt text for your photo, or for every slide in a carousel, ready to paste.

  1. Look at your photoCarousel? Start with slide 1.
  2. Fill in the boxesOnly box 1 is required. The rest make it better.
  3. Copy and pasteInto the app's alt text box. Where's the box?

No AI and no uploads. Nothing you type leaves this page.

Rather have AI write it?

Copy this prompt, open ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Meta AI, paste it, and attach your photo. Check what it writes before you post. You know what's in the picture better than it does.

How the builder works


    

  • It puts your answers in order: what's in the photo, the one detail, why it's there, then the words on it.
    • 

  • It removes "image of," hashtags and emoji. Screen readers already say it's an image and read hashtags and emoji out loud, which slows people down.
    • 

  • It filters profanity and slurs, and the AI prompt asks for clean wording too.
    • 



Next step: paste your alt text into the app. Not sure where the box is? Guide 09 shows you on every app.
