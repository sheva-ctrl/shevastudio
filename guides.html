The 5-Step Workflow Habit

1

Start a fresh conversation

Open your tool and begin a clean slate — context doesn't carry over between distinct sessions.

2

Give it clear context

State your primary goal, target audience, and any constraints upfront. The more specific you are, the sharper the output.

3

Add your source material

Paste explicit text, upload documents, or share approved asset links. Work from your own validated systems.

4

Use it for everyday tasks

Draft quick emails, summarize dense documents, brainstorm strategic content ideas, compare choices, or frame operational outlines.

5

Review before you use it

AI can make errors. Always track and verify metrics, logic, figures, and sources before deploying or acting on any output.

Always Remember

Watch · 1:57

How to Use AI at Work Without Losing Your Judgment

✓

You own the final output

Verify facts, numbers, and sources before sharing or acting on anything an AI tells you.

🔒

Keep sensitive data private

No passwords, personal data, or confidential content unless your IT policy explicitly allows it.

!

AI can be wrong

Treat every output as a starting draft. Ask for sources, push back, use your own judgment.
