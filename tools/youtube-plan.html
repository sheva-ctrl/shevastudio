YouTube Build Plan

12 weeks, Nov 23 to Feb 14. Two long videos and five Shorts a week, built from the Sora Archive, your AI series and your Facebook AI carousels. Long videos build watch hours.

  1. Now38 subscribers. Best Shorts: about 1.6K views each. A 4:29 long video: 69 views.
  2. Step 1 · 500 subscribersAlso needs 3 public uploads in 90 days and either 3,000 watch hours or 3 million Shorts views. Unlocks memberships and Shopping.
  3. Step 2 · 1,000 subscribersPlus 8,000 watch hours in a year (20 million Shorts views is the other route). Unlocks ad revenue for new applicants after Feb 1, 2027.

Rough math, my estimate: at about 3 minutes watched per view, 3,000 hours is about 60,000 long-video views. With Shorts topping out near 1.6K views, the Shorts route (3 to 20 million) is not the path. The Jan 31, 2027 window needs 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 hours already in hand, which takes a breakout video or two on top of steady posting. Treat it as upside, and keep the steady climb as the plan. Shorts views do not count toward watch hours.
