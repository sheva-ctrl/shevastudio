Let AI Plan Your Thanksgiving Oven Timeline

Long video · about 8 minutes · screen recording plus you on camera
Goal for the viewer: by the end, you will have a printed, minute-by-minute oven plan for your Thanksgiving dinner, made in about five minutes.
0:00 · Hook say it fast

You have one oven and four dishes that all want 350 degrees.

That is not a cooking problem. That is a math problem. And AI is good at math problems.

On screenText: "1 oven. 4 dishes. 350°."
ShotClose on camera, standing at the oven. No intro. Straight in.
0:20 · The problem about 40 seconds

Every year it goes the same way. The turkey takes forever. Everything else wants the oven right at the end. And somebody always eats lukewarm rolls.

[Your real Thanksgiving story here. One true detail beats three general ones.]

So this year I'm not doing the math. AI is. I'll show you exactly how.

1:00 · What to give it about 1 minute

AI can only plan with what you tell it. It needs five things.

The five things

  1. Your dishes
  2. How long each one cooks, and at what temperature
  3. How many ovens you have
  4. What else can cook: stovetop, air fryer, slow cooker
  5. When you want to eat
On screenThe five things as a list, one line appearing per beat.

Pull the cook times from your recipes. Not from AI's memory. That matters. I'll come back to it.

2:00 · The prompt show it, then read it

Here's the whole prompt. Paste your menu first. Then type this.

"Here's my menu, cook times and number of ovens. Work backward from 4 PM and give me a timeline with oven temps."
On screenScreen recording. Paste your real menu, then the prompt. Zoom in so the text is readable on a phone.

Work backward from 4 PM. That one phrase is the trick. It starts at dinner and counts back, so nothing lands late.

3:00 · Watch it work about 2 minutes

Now watch what it gives me.

[Read the real timeline out loud, in short pieces. Point at the turkey, then the sides.]

What a good plan has

  • Times that count back from dinner
  • An oven temperature next to every step
  • A rest time for the turkey
  • The turkey out of the oven before the sides go in
  • Anything that can't share the oven moved to the stovetop or air fryer
On screenHighlight each time as you read it. Add a small label: "oven out," "oven in," "rest."
5:00 · Check it do not skip this

Here's the part that matters. AI can be wrong.

So I check three things. Every time.

Three checks

  1. Cook times: do they match my recipes?
  2. The turkey: I cook it until a thermometer reads 165°F in the thickest part, not until the plan says it's done.
  3. The oven: does the plan ever ask for two temperatures at once?

If something is off, just tell it. Type: "The stuffing needs 45 minutes, not 30. Fix the timeline." It redoes the whole thing.

On screenShow one correction and the updated timeline.
6:15 · The ADHD part make it yours

Now here's why this works for my brain.

A plan in my head disappears. A plan on the fridge doesn't.

So I print it and tape it up. Then I set a phone alarm for every step. I don't have to remember anything. The alarm remembers for me.

ShotTaping the printed timeline to the fridge. Show the alarm list on your phone.
7:15 · Wrap about 45 seconds

What we covered

  • Give AI your menu, cook times, ovens and dinner time.
  • Ask it to work backward from dinner.
  • Check the times against your recipes, and the turkey with a thermometer.
