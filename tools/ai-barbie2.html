HOOK (0:00–0:03)
ADHD brain won't start? Same. Here are 7 ShevaStudio-approved injects.

1. Body reset (0:03–0:10)
Move first. Think later. Stand up, shake out, walk the hallway. Motion is ignition.

2. Microscopic starts (0:10–0:17)
Shrink the task until it's almost a joke. Not finish the deck — just open the file.

3. Sound cues (0:17–0:24)
One playlist per purpose. Writing. Cleaning. Gym. Let the audio boss your brain around.

4. Beat the song (0:24–0:31)
Pick a track. Finish something before it ends. Gamify the first inch.

5. Breadcrumbs (0:31–0:38)
Leave the tab open. Write the literal next step. Make tomorrow frictionless.

6. Start at 7:07 (0:38–0:45)
Begin at 7:07 or 3:41. Weird times are harder to ignore than clean ones.

7. Domino the start (0:45–0:52)
Light, movement, music, or one tiny task before your phone steals the wheel.
