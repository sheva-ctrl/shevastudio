#VendorLockIn

Get every vendor fully locked in for your event — without holding it all in your head.

Built for planning brains that juggle a lot. Do one small thing, check it off, walk away. Nothing here has to happen in order, and none of it has to finish today. Your progress saves itself.

How to use this

Three steps. That's the whole thing.

  1. Pick one vendor below. Just one — the one already on your mind.
  2. Work down its 6 boxes. Tap each one only when it's truly done, not "almost."
  3. Stop whenever you want. It saves. A half-checked vendor is still real progress.

What "locked in" actually means

Same 6 things for every vendor — so "done" is never a guess.

Every vendor below uses this exact checklist. When all six are true, they're locked.

  • 1Contract signed — by both of you, not just sent.
  • 2Deposit paid, and you have the receipt.
  • 3Date and times in writing (email counts).
  • 4Exactly what they're doing, in writing — the scope.
  • 5Their contact saved in your phone by name + role.
  • 6Added to your master planning doc or calendar.
