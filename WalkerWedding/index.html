Wedding Party Guidance ◇

Metals & Hardware: All metallic tones are locked to Silver and White Gold only (candlesticks, frames, tableware, and jewelry accents) to cleanly frame the alexandrite stone profiles.

Matron of Honor & Best Man Note: Layout visuals lean completely on the Ivory, Soft Teal, and Nailhead Gray structures. Use deep Plum / Violet tones selectively for flattering statement items or floral coordinate textures.
